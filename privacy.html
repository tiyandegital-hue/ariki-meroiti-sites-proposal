Privacy Policy

This page explains what personal information this website collects and why, in plain language. It follows the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

  • Quote enquiries: your name, phone, email, suburb and project details — only what you type into the contact form, and only so we can respond to your enquiry.
  • Nothing else. This demo site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no accounts or tracking.

How we use it

Enquiry details are used solely to respond to your request for a quote or site visit. We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Third-party services

Fonts load from Google Fonts' servers (fonts.googleapis.com), which may log a standard web request. No other third-party scripts, maps, videos or trackers load on this site.

Your rights

You may ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us via the Contact page and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference and loss. No method of internet transmission is perfectly secure, and we will notify you if a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, as required by law.
